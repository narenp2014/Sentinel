Performance reviews applies to fictional Sentinel employees. Managers hold regular performance conversations and share role-related expectations. Employees should check the current HR portal for location-specific guidance and ask HR when a situation is not covered.

Request handling for performance is reviewed consistently. Managers should explain the relevant process, record decisions in approved systems, and avoid collecting information unrelated to the request.

Employees can contact their manager or the People Operations team for help with performance. HR will explain eligibility, required forms, deadlines, and any applicable local variations before a decision is made.

The performance process should be applied fairly across comparable roles. Exceptions require documented review by the appropriate manager and HR contact; this guide does not create an exception by itself.

Personal information shared during an HR process should be limited to staff who need it to perform their role. Employees should use approved channels and avoid sharing another person's private details.

For planning, employees should submit complete information early and keep a copy of confirmations. The performance guidance is reviewed periodically and may be updated by an official notice from HR.

If two sources appear inconsistent, the current official policy and applicable law take precedence. The assistant may summarize this document but should not invent an entitlement, promise an outcome, or provide binding legal advice.

Common questions about performance should be directed to HR when the answer depends on an employee's location, employment status, or individual circumstances. HR can review the specific situation through authorized procedures.

Before submitting a performance request, employees should review the current instructions, include relevant dates, and explain the work-related context. A complete request helps the responsible reviewer understand what decision is needed without asking for unnecessary personal details.

A manager reviewing performance should apply the same published criteria to comparable situations. If the request needs an exception, the manager should explain the reason, consult HR, and wait for approval before communicating a final decision.

Employees are responsible for checking that forms and supporting materials for performance are accurate. If a document contains an error, contact the responsible HR team and request a correction rather than submitting conflicting versions through multiple channels.

When a performance request is pending, employees may ask the assigned contact for a status update. A status update is not an approval, and employees should not assume a request has been granted until they receive confirmation through the normal process.

The company reviews performance guidance as business practices and local requirements change. HR owns official updates and should communicate effective dates. Older copies may be out of date, so employees should use the current published version.

For a question not answered here, share only the minimum information needed with HR. The assistant can summarize general guidance but cannot see every employee's eligibility, approve a request, disclose confidential records, or guarantee a particular outcome.

Records related to performance should be stored only in approved company systems with access limited to staff who need them. Do not copy another employee's personal information into a general chat, shared document, or unrelated request.
